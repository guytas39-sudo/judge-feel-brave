A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — one quiet evening scene, no instructions, no slogans.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 stdlib only) writes the text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt`), UTF-8, so both runs produce identical bytes. The text is a small scene: a kettle cooling, a river carrying the light downstream, a lit lamp and a warm chair — the kind of evening where nothing needs deciding yet.

The idea is simple: the text never tells its reader to calm down. It offers a room where time has already slowed — the river takes the light away and returns it as evening, the kettle ticks, the cat sleeps on the rug — so the steadiness arrives on its own, as something noticed rather than something ordered. Bravery, here, is just the quiet knowledge that nothing in this room is urgent.
